## **1st World Preset - Winter World**

Many of you may remember this feature from the old Minecraft Alpha versions. Well it's back now with **more challenging** features! 

The whole world is buried in snow and in a never-ending winter cycle.

But now there is a new concern to watch out for. Introducing a **temperature bar**! 

![Replace this with a description](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/26c240431af939a2c18c24b34ddb361b2fb13fda.png)

If you go **under -10°C** you start **freezing**. Swimming underwater, standing near a heat source, the daytime cycle and even the weather **all effect your temperature**. Also there are **blizzards**, so watch out!

**A true Minecraft classic. A singular block which goes through many phases, until you reach the end**. Of course there are chests with custom loot tables, and random mobs for phases. Can you make till the end?

## **4th World Preset - Skygrid World**

**Absolute chaos of random blocks. Original by SethBling.**

In this world preset there are random blocks everywhere spaced by 2 blocks horizontally and 3 blocks vertically from each other. Random spawners and chests with random loot tables occasionally spawn. This may be quite **CPU heavy**!

![Skygrid world](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/813d0b6f115b0b9cbcc3485ca0c52a55e78bc6c5.png)

## **5/6/7/8th World Preset - Challenge Worlds**

**There are 4 challenge worlds, each being very different from each other.**

## **Challenge world 1**

In this world you spawn at the very bottom of the world with a **bonus chest**! There is no bedrock and the surface is located at **Y=320**. If you die you respawn at the bottom, I'd recommend setting a spawnpoint with a **bed**. **Good luck!**

![Challenge World 1](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/a08b7a9b25e5f3c82c2d7b9cae4d3c427e6889b1.png)

## **Challenge world 2**

In this world you spawn in a random wooden structure. The catch? **The world is nothing but only structures**. All structures can spawn, no limits. **Strongholds also spawn**, so the game is beatable!

![Replace this with a description](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/cafbc2fc66852214abce2e3c4e007c336fd432f8_0.webp)

## **Challenge world 3**

Here the world generation is normal. But your total health is **cut in half (5 hearts max)**, food is **cut in half (5 food icons)**, you can only use **half of the inventory** and you **can't sneak** (the cherry on top). Good Luck trying to beat Minecraft this way!

![Replace this with a description](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/1d6159a24f4cd9ec0beff2e1d35376441598cdfc.png)

![Replace this with a description](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/c849be660afae7650d26b94ca3b0bbe878e8fc81.png)

## **Challenge world 4**

Here the chunk dimesions are **11x11, with wide void gaps**. Nice and simple. **Hard to traverse and find structures**. Good Luck on beating the game!
